# Ubion competitor monitoring

Create a decision-useful Korean competitive-intelligence brief from the user's
Papr subscriptions and, when current coverage requires it, credible public web
sources. Prioritize **원티드랩, 엘리스그룹, 클라썸, 프리윌린, 플리토**. Treat the
companies as priorities, not an exclusive list: include another organisation
only when it materially affects Ubion's education-business position.

## Collect evidence

Start with `papr` to orient, then refresh and inspect the relevant articles.
Use `papr read` for candidate articles; request `--full` or run `papr extract`
only when the feed summary does not establish the claim. Use the article URL,
publication date, and publisher returned by Papr in the brief.

If the subscriptions cannot cover the requested period or entity, use web
research for primary sources and reputable reporting. Do not present the result
as comprehensive when the source coverage is incomplete. Do not subscribe to
new feeds, change article state, or alter tags unless the user asks.

Group wire copies and press-release republications into one event. Keep the
primary announcement as the lead source and note meaningful independent
reporting separately. Exclude routine hiring posts, generic event notices, and
unsubstantiated marketing unless they reveal a relevant strategic signal.

## Determine relevance

Prioritize events involving one or more of these signals:

- LMS, LXP, AI learning product, feature, pricing, or free-tier changes
- university, enterprise, public-sector contract, tender, procurement, or new customer
- government project selection, public-private partnership, MOU, regulation, or policy
- funding, M&A, IPO, earnings, retrenchment, executive change, or key hire
- overseas expansion, international-student services, ODA, or global partnership
- business entry, exit, positioning change, or direct mention/comparison of Ubion

Map each event to the likely Ubion business area. State only an evidence-based
connection; label a plausible but unverified implication as an inference.

## Write the brief

Use Korean. Begin with a short executive summary containing the three most
material changes, immediate threats, and actionable opportunities. Then list
each event in this format:

1. **Event** — company, title, publication date, publisher/channel, source URL
2. **Facts and competitor message** — separate reported facts from the company's framing
3. **Coverage tone** — favourable, neutral, or critical, with a short reason
4. **Product or capability gap** — a capability, delivery model, or differentiation Ubion does not clearly offer; use “확인 불가” when this cannot be established
5. **Industry and policy link** — relevant market, regulation, or programme signal
6. **Competitive meaning** — expansion signal, threat level (high/medium/low), and the affected Ubion business area
7. **Recommended response** — a concrete next check, product, sales, partnership, or policy action; distinguish recommendation from fact

Finish with a compact table of threats, opportunities, owner candidate, and
next evidence to collect. Explicitly state “중요 신규 신호 없음” for a priority
company with no material finding rather than silently omitting it.

## Evidence rules

- Link every event to its original article or announcement and preserve its publication date.
- Prefer the organisation, customer, government agency, investor filing, or procurement record over a secondary summary when claims conflict.
- Never infer a contract award, funding amount, product availability, or Ubion capability from an ambiguous announcement.
- Report direct comparisons or mentions of Ubion verbatim only when short and necessary; otherwise summarize them.
